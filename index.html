Before you go

Questions, answered

The five things people ask most.
Everything else lives in the full Terms & Conditions.

Do I have to trade to get the cashback?

Only a little — half a qualifying lot. Close 0.5 qualifying lots on the eligible products by 31 October, keep at least $50 net deposit in your account for the 30 days after your first deposit, and the cashback is yours.
Do I have to opt in before I deposit?

Yes, and it is the one step people miss. Opt in first, then deposit. A deposit made before you opt in does not count toward the promotion and cannot be backdated.
How much do I get back?

100% of your first deposit, returned as real cash, up to a maximum of $10. Once it is credited there is no trading requirement and no withdrawal restriction — trade it or take it out.
What counts as one qualifying lot?

One platform lot of gold, silver or platinum equals one qualifying lot. For indices, energy and Bitcoin, ten platform lots make one qualifying lot.

You can build a qualifying lot from as many small trades as you like — hold each position for at least two minutes, and open and close it inside the promotion window.
How does the getaway draw work?

You get 1 entry the moment you make a qualifying first deposit, then 25 more entries for every whole qualifying lot you close through 31 October. There is no cap.

One winner is drawn at random from all entries, every entry carrying an equal chance, and announced on this page.
